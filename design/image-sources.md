# Image sources (design/mockups/img/)

All three images are paintings by Rabindranath Tagore (1861–1941), in the public domain (the author died more than
60 years ago under Indian law and more than 80 years ago generally). They are the same downscaled copies (≤560 px wide,
JPEG, under 150 KB each) that the Anandadhara repository holds in `docs/img/`, taken from Wikimedia Commons; the Commons
file pages state the public-domain status with the templates listed below.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| seven-figures.jpg | Rabindranath Tagore Seven Figures.jpg | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rabindranath_Tagore_Seven_Figures.jpg | Rabindranath Tagore, before 1941 (NGMA, New Delhi) | PD-Art (PD-old-auto), PD-old-80, CC-PD-Mark | Concept A: faint luminosity texture inside the mood tiles |
| bird-fantastic.jpg | Rabindranath Tagore Bird Fantastic.jpg | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rabindranath_Tagore_Bird_Fantastic.jpg | Rabindranath Tagore, before 1941 (NGMA, New Delhi) | PD-Art (PD-old-auto), PD-old-80, CC-PD-Mark | Concept C: the Anandadhara record sleeve |

Files marked "NGMA" are reproductions of paintings held by the National Gallery of Modern Art, New Delhi, uploaded to
Commons as faithful reproductions of two-dimensional public-domain works (PD-Art). The licence templates were read from
each file page on 5 October 2026 (see the Anandadhara repository's `design/image-sources.md`); please re-check the file
pages before any public use beyond these mockups. Concept B uses no raster images (its scene is drawn in SVG); the app
icons are rendered from `mockups/icons.html` (SVG + Google Fonts) and contain no third-party artwork.
